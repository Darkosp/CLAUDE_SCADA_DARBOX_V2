## The three open defects, in the order they should be taken

**1. A new symbol assumes a boolean tag and is silent when it is not.** §2.0l. An author who binds a
numeric tag gets a symbol that draws `stopped` forever with no warning, because `is true` never matches a
number and the fallback is always taken. **This is the one to do first**: it is the only one that
produces a *wrong reading on a screen* rather than a missing feature.

**2. Nothing marks a value that cannot be true.** §2.0l. `464 %` is drawn like `4.79 bar`. Needs a
decision before code — whether the product should have an opinion, and whose it would be.

**3. The `discrete` kind has no editor.** Older than this session, recorded so it is not rediscovered.

## The one thing most likely to cost the next session time

**PowerShell writes are not UTF-8 here, and the damage is invisible.**

`Set-Content` and here-strings decoded UTF-8 as Windows-1252 and re-encoded it, so each byte of a
multi-byte character became a character of its own. `Write…` became `Write` plus three stray characters
**on a button in the running application**, and `§` was damaged eleven times in `screen.ts` alone.

**The damage is VALID UTF-8**, so it compiles, it passes, and nothing fails — it renders as nonsense.

Three rules follow, and all three were learned the hard way inside this one session:

1. **Do not rewrite a source file through PowerShell.** Use the editor tools, or Node.
2. **A check written in terms of decoded characters cannot be trusted about bytes that fail to decode.**
   A scan that searched for the text of a damaged `·` reported **fourteen healthy files**, because
   latin1 cannot represent `·` and the search matched its *correct* UTF-8.
3. **Verify the whole result, not the part you changed.** A repair carried a lone high byte forward
   unchanged while looking like it had succeeded, turning `ADR-0024 §9` into `ADR-0024 <0xA7>9`.

**The guard now exists and it works**: `src/Web/tests/encoding.test.mjs` fails the suite on damaged text
or invalid UTF-8 anywhere in `src`, `docs` or `tools`. It caught real damage **the same day it was
written**, in the very file that introduced it. If it fails, run `node tools/repair-encoding.mjs <files>`.

## Habits this session confirmed

- **A screenshot is not the application.** Every defect worth finding here came from *looking at a
  running screen*: the tile caption, the timestamp on every tile, the fixed reading size, the missing
  `Edit tag` control, the stretched write button, the mojibake on a button. None was visible from code.
- **An instruction that cannot be carried out is a defect in the instruction.** The walk's own step 4
  told the reader to tick a box on a form that did not exist, and the person following it could not tell
  a missing control from their own mistake.
- **Say when something is unfixed.** Three defects are open and are written down above rather than left
  in a conversation.
